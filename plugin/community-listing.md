## Name

Layer Bloom

## Tagline

Turn "Frame 58" into names that make sense. AI suggests, you decide.

## Description

Your layers panel deserves better than Frame 58, Group 12 and Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 9.48 PM.

Layer Bloom looks inside your frames (the text, sizes, layout and, if you like, a quick screenshot) and suggests clear names. Nothing changes until you say so. One click renames everything you ticked, and one Cmd+Z undoes it all.

What you get:

- Use your own AI: OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or OpenRouter
- Pick a naming style: Title Case, Slash/Paths or kebab-case
- Edit or skip any suggestion, and click a name to jump to that layer
- Components stay safe, with no messy overrides
- Works on any Figma plan
- Light and dark mode

Your API key stays on your computer. Layer info goes straight to the AI you pick and never to me.

Free and open source. Questions or ideas? rachelkenyani@gmail.com
